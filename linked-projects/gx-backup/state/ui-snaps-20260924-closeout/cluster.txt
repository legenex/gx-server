Cluster
Two separate computers. Two independent 128 GB unified-memory systems (about 121 GiB usable each). They do NOT share memory: there is no 256 GB pool. Each node runs its own models inside its own memory. Only gx-max spans both, as two tensor-parallel ranks that exchange activations over the ConnectX-7 RoCE rails.
